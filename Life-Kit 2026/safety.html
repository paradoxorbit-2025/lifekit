Road & Traffic Safety
▼
1

Helmet & Seatbelt — Always

No exception. Helmet reduces fatal head injury by 70%. Seatbelt keeps you in vehicle during collision — ejection is almost always fatal.

2

No Phone While Moving

Phone use while driving increases accident risk 4x — same as drunk driving. If you must call, pull over and stop completely.

3

Cross at Zebra Crossings

Look right, left, right again. Make eye contact with driver before stepping. Don't assume they've seen you.

4

Never Drink & Drive

Even 1 drink affects reaction time and judgment. Take auto/cab — no debate, no exception.

5

Be Visible at Night

Light-colored clothes, reflectors, or torch if walking on dark roads. Assume drivers cannot see you until proven otherwise.

💡

Share live location when traveling at night. 5 seconds of effort that can save your life.

📵
Mobile During Driving — Why It Kills
▼
1

Reaction Time Doubles

At 60 km/h, a 2-second phone distraction means you travel 33 meters blind. That's enough to miss a child crossing.

2

Handsfree is Not Safe Either

Handsfree calls still distract your brain — it's the conversation, not just the hand holding the phone. Your attention is split.

3

Use DND While Driving

Enable "Do Not Disturb while Driving" in iPhone (Focus) or Android settings. Auto-replies keep your contacts informed.

School Bus & Child Safety
▼
1

Teach Bus Safety

Wait 3 big steps from kerb. Never cross behind the bus. Wait for driver signal before crossing in front.

2

Stranger Danger Basics

Teach children: no adult needs help from a child. If uncomfortable, yell loudly "THIS IS NOT MY PARENT" and run to nearest crowd.

3

Safe Words & Check-In

Establish a family safe word. Child should know: home address, parent phone number, and safe adults to approach.

Cybersecurity & Online Safety
▼
1

Strong Unique Passwords

Different password for every account. Use passphrase: "BlueCat$RunsFast2024" — easy to remember, hard to crack.

2

Never Share OTP

No real bank, government, or company will ever ask for your OTP. Ever. Hang up immediately. Don't feel embarrassed.

3

Enable 2FA Everywhere

Turn on Two-Factor Authentication for WhatsApp, email, banking. Even if password stolen, they can't log in.

4

Phishing Links

Look at actual URL before clicking — "hdfc-bank-alert.com" is not HDFC. When in doubt, open the real site manually.

5

Public Wi-Fi

Never do banking or log into accounts on public Wi-Fi. Use mobile data for anything sensitive.

⚠️

Cyber fraud? Call 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline) or report at cybercrime.gov.in immediately — faster action = more recovery chance.

Digital Privacy & Social Media
▼
1

Don't Share Location Publicly

Real-time location stories (train, hotel check-ins) tell burglars your home is empty. Share after you return.

2

Audit App Permissions

Regularly check which apps have camera, microphone, location access. Revoke anything that doesn't need it.

3

Right to Be Forgotten

You can request Google to remove personal info from search results (Google's "Results About You" tool). Works for old photos, addresses etc.

🎭
Deepfake & Identity Theft
▼
1

Recognize Deepfakes

Look for: unnatural blinking, hair edges, ear details, teeth looking off, background distortion. When in doubt, verify via different channel.

2

Voice Cloning Scams

"Family emergency" calls using cloned voice are growing. Establish a family code word — if they don't say it, it's a scam.

3

Limit Public Photos

Fewer public photos = less training data for deepfakes. Make social media private. Review tagged photos regularly.

4

Identity Stolen?

File complaint at cybercrime.gov.in + local police. Inform bank, change all passwords, enable credit monitoring.

Smart Home & IoT Security
▼
1

Change Default Passwords

Router, CCTV camera, smart doorbell — all come with default passwords that are publicly known. Change them on day one.

2

Keep Firmware Updated

Smart device updates patch security holes. Enable auto-update for all IoT devices.

3

Indoor Cameras Carefully

Never place cameras in bedrooms or bathrooms regardless of brand. If hacked, footage is on the internet — permanently.

Home Security
▼
1

Lock Up Before Sleep

Ground floor windows are primary entry points. Always lock before sleeping. Grills add extra protection.

2

Verify Before Opening Door

Don't open to strangers. Legitimate officials carry ID and won't mind you asking. Call their office to verify if unsure.

3

Lighting Matters

Well-lit entry and surroundings deter thieves. Motion-sensor lights are cheap and effective.

4

Valuables Out of Sight

Don't keep valuables visible from windows. Don't post travel plans on social media before leaving — signals empty home.

Kitchen & Electrical Safety
▼
1

Never Wet Hands Near Switches

Dry hands completely before touching switches or plugs. A fraction of a second's complacency can cause electrocution.

2

Unplug When Not in Use

Iron, charger, heater — unplug when done. Standby power causes fires. Don't overload extension boards.

3

Oil Fire — Never Use Water

Water on oil fire causes explosive splattering. Smother with a lid or wet cloth. Turn off gas source. Never move burning oil pan.

4

Gas Cylinder Safety

Check rubber pipe every 6 months for cracks. Check expiry date on metal ring of cylinder. Never use near open flame when connecting.

Home Fire Safety & Prevention
▼
1

Install Smoke Alarm

Place in bedroom, kitchen, hallway. Test monthly. Replace batteries yearly. A smoke alarm doubles your survival chance.

2

Plan Escape Routes

Know 2 ways out of every room. Practice with family. Decide meeting point outside. Do this before a fire happens.

3

Keep Fire Extinguisher

ABC-type extinguisher handles most home fires. Mount near kitchen exit (not inside kitchen — fire may block access).

🛗
Elevator / Lift Safety
▼
1

If Lift Stops

Stay calm. Press alarm button and call button. Call building security or emergency number on panel. Never try to force doors open.

2

Never Jump Inside Lift

Jumping can trigger safety sensors and stop the lift. Never try to climb out of a stuck lift — drop zones between floors are fatal.

3

Never Use During Fire

Power outage during fire = stuck in a metal box filling with smoke. Always use stairs during fire evacuation.

Women's Safety
▼
1

Trust Your Instincts

If something feels wrong — it is. Leave immediately. You don't need proof. Your gut is a survival tool.

2

Share Live Location

When going out alone — especially at night — share live location with trusted person. Update when you reach safely.

3

Emergency Contacts on Speed Dial

Set police (100), Women's Helpline (1091), and trusted contact as quick-dial. Know how to dial without looking at phone.

4

If Followed

Don't go home — go to crowded public place, police station, or hospital. Make eye contact with bystanders. Shout if needed.

5

Auto/Cab Safety

Share cab details (number plate, driver name) with someone. Sit behind driver — not passenger side. Trust your read of the driver.

💡

Nirbhaya Fund resources: Women's Helpline 181, Domestic Violence 181, Sexual Harassment 1091 — all free, 24/7.

Night Travel Safety
▼
1

Plan Your Route

Know the route before you leave. Stick to lit, populated roads. Avoid shortcuts through isolated areas even if faster.

2

Be Aware, Not Distracted

Earphones block situational awareness. Keep volume low or one ear free. Be aware of who's around you.

3

Walk Confidently

Head up, purposeful stride. Looking lost or unsure marks you as a target. Project that you know exactly where you're going.

4

Check In When Safe

Send a "home safe" message. Apps like bSafe and Noonlight have automatic check-in and SOS features.

Stalker Safety
▼
1

Go to Crowded Place

If followed — go to a busy shop, police station, hospital, or any crowded area. Don't go home or to an isolated place.

2

Vary Your Routine

Change routes, timings. Don't be predictable. Stalkers rely on patterns to find you.

3

Document Everything

Screenshot messages, save dates/times of incidents. This is evidence for police FIR under IPC Section 354D (Stalking).

4

Report to Police

Stalking is a cognizable offence — police must register FIR. If they refuse, approach Magistrate directly. Don't ignore it.

Ragging & Bullying — College/School
▼
1

Know It's Illegal

Ragging is a criminal offence in India. UGC Anti-Ragging regulations mandate expulsion and imprisonment for perpetrators.

2

Report Immediately

Anti-Ragging Helpline: 1800-180-5522 (free). Report to college Anti-Ragging Committee. You're protected — your name is kept confidential.

3

For Bullying

Document incidents (screenshots, dates). Report to school counselor or principal. If physical — file police complaint. Don't normalize it.

Dating App Safety
▼
1

First Meeting — Public Place Always

Coffee shop, restaurant, mall — never private location. Tell a friend where you're going and who you're meeting.

2

Don't Share Personal Details Early

Don't give last name, home address, workplace, or daily routine to someone you haven't met in person and verified.

3

Reverse Image Search

Google reverse image search profile photos — catfishing is common. Real people don't typically use model-quality photos.

4

Trust Your Read

If something feels off — leave. You don't owe anyone your time or an explanation. Safety first, politeness second.

Child Safety — Good Touch/Bad Touch
▼
1

Teach Body Autonomy

Teach children: their body belongs to them. They can say NO to any touch — even from relatives or trusted adults — if uncomfortable.

2

Safe vs Unsafe Touch

Safe: handshake, hug they initiate. Unsafe: touch on private parts, touches that make them feel scared or confused.

3

Tell a Safe Adult

Children must know: if someone touches them wrongly — tell a trusted adult immediately. They will not be in trouble.

4

POCSO Act

Any sexual act with a child under 18 is a crime under POCSO — even if child "consented." Penalty: 7 years to life imprisonment.

Public Transport Safety
▼
1

Guard Belongings

Keep bag in front. Inner pockets for phone/wallet. Don't display expensive items. Pickpockets target distracted commuters.

2

Know Emergency Exits

When boarding train or bus — note nearest exit. In emergency, knowing this saves seconds that matter.

3

Trust Your Instincts

If someone makes you uncomfortable — move. Change seats, compartments, or get off at next stop. Don't wait to be sure.

Solo Travel Safety
▼
1

Research Before You Go

Know: local scams, safe areas, unsafe areas, local emergency numbers, nearest Indian Embassy address.

2

Register with Embassy

For international travel: register at indianembassy.org or MEA portal. They can reach you in emergencies.

3

Common Scams

"Friendly local" offering too much help, fake taxis, gem scams, "closed today" redirections, distraction theft — research specific destination scams.

4

Digital Safety Abroad

Use VPN on hotel/café Wi-Fi. Keep offline maps downloaded. Don't store hotel room number in phone case with key card.

Camping & Trekking Safety
▼
1

Tell Someone Your Plan

Share exact route, destination, and expected return time with someone responsible. Check in at intervals.

2

If Lost

STOP: Stop, Think, Observe, Plan. Stay put — moving while lost makes rescue harder. Use whistle (3 blasts = distress). Find high ground for signal.

3

Wild Animal Encounter

Don't run — triggers chase instinct. Back away slowly. Make yourself look larger. Don't make sudden movements. Specific behavior varies by animal — research beforehand.

4

Weather Changes

Mountain weather can change in minutes. If dark clouds build — descend immediately. Hypothermia and lightning are real risks.

Swimming Pool & Beach Safety
▼
1

Rip Currents

Don't swim against a rip current — you'll exhaust yourself. Swim parallel to shore until out of current, then swim back at angle.

2

Swim Near Lifeguard

Always swim in designated areas with active lifeguard. Red flag = no swimming. Respect flags — they save lives.

3

Pool Rules

No running. No diving in shallow end (causes spinal injuries). Supervision for children at all times — drowning is silent, not splashy.

ATM & Banking Safety
▼
1

Cover Your PIN Always

Shield keypad with your hand even when alone. Cameras can be placed anywhere. This is a permanent habit, not situational.

2

Check for Skimmer

Wiggle the card slot — a skimmer overlay will be loose. Check for extra camera above keypad. If suspicious — don't use, report to bank.

3

Use Bank ATMs

Standalone ATMs in isolated areas are higher risk. Bank branch ATMs are monitored and maintained more carefully.

4

Enable SMS Alerts

Turn on transaction SMS for every rupee. Immediate notification = immediate action if fraudulent transaction occurs.

Crypto & Investment Scams
▼
1

Red Flags

Guaranteed returns, WhatsApp investment groups, "insider tips," celebrity endorsements, pressure to invest quickly — all are scam signals.

2

Verify SEBI Registration

Any legitimate investment advisor in India must be SEBI registered. Check at sebi.gov.in before giving a single rupee.

3

Fake Apps

Scam apps let you "see" growing profits but block withdrawal. Only use officially listed apps from SEBI-registered platforms.

⚠️

If scammed: report to cybercrime.gov.in + call 1930 immediately. Time matters — faster reports increase money recovery chances.

Workplace Ergonomics & Safety
▼
1

Correct Posture

Screen at eye level. Back straight, feet flat on floor. Elbows at 90°. Poor posture causes chronic spinal and neck injury over time.

2

20-20-20 Rule for Eyes

Every 20 minutes: look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds. Prevents digital eye strain and headaches.

3

Machinery Precautions

Never bypass safety guards. Lockout/Tagout before maintenance. Report faulty equipment immediately — don't use and warn others.

Food Safety
▼
1

Danger Zone Temperature

Bacteria multiply rapidly between 5°C–60°C. Don't leave cooked food at room temperature more than 2 hours — especially in summer.

2

Check Expiry Dates

"Best before" = quality. "Use by" = safety. Never consume past "use by" date regardless of how it looks or smells.

3

Street Food Safety

Choose stalls with high turnover (food cooked fresh, not sitting). Avoid raw cut fruits in unhygienic conditions. Watch if vendor washes hands.

4

Food Poisoning Response

Hydrate with ORS constantly. Seek hospital if: blood in stool, fever above 39°C, symptoms in elderly/children/pregnant, no improvement in 48 hrs.

Mental Health Crisis — Recognizing & Helping
▼
1

Warning Signs

Sudden withdrawal, giving away possessions, drastic mood changes, talking about being a burden, hopelessness, reckless behavior.

2

Ask Directly

"Are you okay? Are you thinking of harming yourself?" — this doesn't plant the idea. It opens a door. Asking shows you care.

3

Listen Without Fixing

Don't offer solutions. Don't minimize. Just listen. "That sounds really hard. I'm here." is more powerful than any advice.

4

Professional Help

iCall: 9152987821 | NIMHANS: 080-46110007 | Vandrevala Foundation: 1860-2662-345 (24/7). Help them make the call if needed.

Drug / Alcohol Emergency Situation
▼
1

Alcohol Overdose

Unconscious, breathing irregularly, cannot be woken = alcohol poisoning. Don't leave alone. Recovery position. Call 112. Cold shower myth is dangerous — don't do it.

2

Unknown Substance Intake

If someone ingests unknown substance: note what/when/how much. Call 112 or Poison Control. Keep them awake and talking if conscious.

3

Help, Not Judgment

If someone is struggling with addiction — guide toward NIMHANS helpline or local de-addiction center. Shame doesn't help recovery. Compassion does.
